Why monitoring matters

A camera on its own is a witness. Not a guard.

Most CCTV only gets watched after something has gone wrong. Live monitoring turns the cameras you already own into an active defence that sees, decides and acts while it still matters.

Recorded is not prevented

Footage usually gets reviewed the morning after. By then the stock is gone, the damage is done and all you have is evidence.

Unverified alarms get ignored

Wind, rain, animals and headlights set off motion alerts all night. After a week of false alarms, nobody responds to the real one.

Guards cover one spot

An on-site guard sees one area for one shift. Your cameras see the whole site at once — they just need someone watching them.
